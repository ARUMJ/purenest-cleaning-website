## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:**
PureNest Cleaning Co.

**Industry:**
Residential & Commercial Cleaning Services

**Location:**
Dallas, Texas, USA

**Business Type:**
Local cleaning company serving residential and commercial customers.

**Website Type:**
Professional marketing and lead-generation website.

**Project Purpose:**
Create a polished, conversion-focused website that demonstrates how a modern cleaning company could use its website to build trust, explain its services, establish local credibility, and generate quote requests.

This is a fictional portfolio/concept project. It must never be presented as a real client or as a real business.

## 2. Business Concept

PureNest Cleaning Co. is a fictional local cleaning company positioned as a dependable, professional cleaning service helping homeowners and businesses maintain clean, comfortable spaces.

**Brand personality:**

- Clean
- Professional
- Reliable
- Friendly
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Approachable
- Premium but not excessively luxurious

**Avoid making the company look cheap or generic.**

## 9. Trust Signals

The website should communicate:

---

## 10. Brand Visual Direction

The visual identity should feel:

- Fresh
- Clean
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Approachable

**Design characteristics:**

- Generous whitespace
- Clean layouts
- Strong typography
- High-quality cleaning/home imagery
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Professional cards
- Clear CTA buttons
- Modern navigation
- Excellent mobile experience

**Avoid:**

- Excessive animations
- Generic AI-looking layouts
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Clutter
- Huge blocks of text
- Competing CTAs

**Initial color direction:**

- Deep blue / navy as primary
- Fresh green / teal as accent
- White / light neutral backgrounds
- Dark charcoal text

> Exact colors will be decided during the UI/UX phase.

---

## 11. Typography

Use a modern, highly readable sans-serif typeface.

Maintain a clear hierarchy between:

- H1
- H2
- H3
- Body
- Supporting text

---

## 12. Content Direction

The website should quickly answer:

- What does PureNest do?
- Where does it operate?
- Who does it serve?
- Why should customers trust it?
- What services are offered?
- How does the process work?
- How can visitors request a quote?

**Initial positioning direction:**

> "Professional Cleaning. A Fresher Space. More Time for You."

**Supporting direction:**

> "Reliable residential and commercial cleaning services designed to give you a cleaner space without adding another task to your schedule."

This copy can be refined later.

---

## 13. Website Success Criteria

Within approximately 5–10 seconds, a visitor should understand:

1. What PureNest does
2. Where it operates
3. Who it serves
4. Why they should consider it
5. What action they should take

---

## 14. Technical Quality Goals

The eventual website must be:

- Fully responsive
- Accessible
- SEO-ready
- Performance-focused
- Production-quality
- Vercel-ready

**Consider:**

- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard accessibility
- Proper contrast
- Alt text
- Accessible forms
- Proper heading hierarchy
- Visible focus states
- Metadata
- Descriptive URLs
- Image optimization
- Performance optimization

---

## 15. Project Constraints

**Version 1 should include:**

- Responsive website
- Navigation
- Services
- About
- Testimonials
- Service areas
- Contact / quote form
- CTA system
- Footer
- Basic SEO
- Accessibility
- Mobile optimization

**Do NOT build initially:**

- Customer login
- Admin dashboard
- Complex booking system
- Payment processing
- Customer database
- Authentication
- Complex backend
- CMS

> The goal is to demonstrate a high-quality professional local-business website, not an enterprise cleaning-management platform.
