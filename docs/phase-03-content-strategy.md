# PureNest Cleaning Co. — Phase 3: Content Strategy & Website Copy

> **Status:** Phase 3 — Content Strategy & Website Copy
> **Phase:** Content Strategy
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-09
> **Project Type:** Fictional portfolio / concept website
> **Disclaimer:** This is a fictional project. It must never be presented as a real client or as a real business.

This document defines the approved content strategy and website copy for PureNest Cleaning Co. It builds on the Phase 1 Project Brief and the Phase 2 Sitemap & Information Architecture, and is the source of truth for all page-level copy, CTAs, FAQ content, navigation labels, and SEO metadata for Version 1.

---

## 1. Content Objective

The primary content objective is to **convert visitors into qualified quote inquiries**.

**Content sequence:**

```
Attention → Relevance → Trust → Understanding → Action
```

The website should communicate the business proposition quickly and clearly.

## 3. Brand Voice

The brand voice should be:

- Professional
- Warm
- Clear
- Confident
- Helpful
- Local
- Customer-focused

**Avoid:**

- Corporate jargon
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Aggressive sales language
- Unrealistic promises
- Unverified "number-one" claims
- Fake statistics
- Fake awards
- Fake certifications
- Generic AI-style copy

## 11. Homepage FAQ

**H2:**
`Frequently Asked Questions`

**Q: What cleaning services does PureNest offer?**
A: PureNest offers residential cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, Airbnb and short-term rental cleaning, and commercial cleaning.

**Q: Do you offer recurring cleaning?**
A: Yes. Recurring cleaning can be requested as part of your service needs. Contact us with details about your space and preferred schedule.

**Q: Do you offer deep cleaning?**
A: Yes. Our deep-cleaning service is designed for spaces that need more detailed attention than routine cleaning.

**Q: Do you clean offices and commercial spaces?**
A: Yes. Commercial cleaning is available for offices and other small business environments.

**Q: Do you offer move-in and move-out cleaning?**
A: Yes. Move-in and move-out cleaning is available for customers preparing a property for a new occupant or leaving a property behind.

**Q: Which areas do you serve?**
A: The PureNest concept currently focuses on Dallas and surrounding communities including Plano, Frisco, Irving, Richardson, and Carrollton.

**Q: How do I request a quote?**
A: Complete our online quote form with a few details about your space and the service you need. We'll use that information to understand your request.

## 25. Content Rules

**DO:**

- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use descriptive headings.
- Make copy scannable.
- Use clear CTAs.
- Maintain consistent terminology.
- Write for customers rather than search engines.
- Preserve fictional-project safeguards.

**DO NOT:**

- Invent statistics.
- Invent awards.
- Invent certifications.
- Invent years of experience.
- Invent real customer reviews.
- Claim real Google ratings.
- Claim real business results.
- Use real businesses' branding.
- Stuff keywords unnaturally.
